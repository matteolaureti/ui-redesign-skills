# Landing page redesign guidance

Use this guidance for product landing pages, SaaS marketing sites, startup homepages, pricing-led pages, and conversion-focused web experiences.

## Priorities

Treat the page as a narrative that helps the visitor understand the product and decide what to do next.

A strong redesign should make these ideas clear in an order appropriate to the product:

- what the product is;
- why it matters to the intended user;
- how it works or what it enables;
- what evidence or trust supports the claim;
- what action the visitor should take.

## Hero

Make the product category and value proposition understandable quickly. Use product UI, photography, diagrams, or other visual evidence when they clarify the offer.

Avoid vague oversized slogans, detached floating dashboard cards, decorative gradients, or generic CTA pairs that do not reflect the product.

## Page structure

Do not automatically reproduce the common AI SaaS sequence of hero, logo strip, three feature cards, bento grid, testimonials, three pricing cards, FAQ accordion, and final CTA.

Choose a structure that fits the product. Useful alternatives include editorial storytelling, split product demonstrations, sticky narratives, workflow explanations, visual comparisons, use-case chapters, integrated proof, and progressive feature storytelling.

Vary section rhythm rather than repeating heading + paragraph + three cards.

## Product evidence and trust

Treat screenshots and product imagery as evidence of capability, not decoration.

Use only believable trust signals supported by the reference or user request. Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, certifications, or performance claims.

If pricing is present, make comparison and audience fit clear rather than presenting three nearly identical towers.

## Avoid

Avoid generic AI illustration, excessive bento cards, meaningless 3D decoration, repeated symmetrical sections, and copy that could belong to any SaaS product.

The final page should feel specific to the product and its audience.
